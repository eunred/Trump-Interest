# Classification taxonomy

Use the narrowest defensible labels. Multiple topics, policy instruments, and industries may apply. Add a new label only when repeated posts cannot be represented faithfully by the existing list; document any additions in the report.

## Korea context

- `south_korea`: Republic of Korea, Seoul, South Korean government, companies, or trade.
- `north_korea`: DPRK, Pyongyang, Kim Jong Un, nuclear or sanctions context clearly tied to North Korea.
- `both_koreas`: Both governments or inter-Korean relations are explicit.
- `korean_peninsula_general`: Korea or the peninsula is discussed without a reliable North/South assignment.
- `ambiguous_korea`: The referent cannot be resolved from the post and linked material.
- `not_applicable`: Steel-only post with no Korea reference.

Never convert bare `Korea` to South Korea solely because the surrounding issue is trade; inspect names, companies, linked material, and contemporaneous context.

## Topics

- `trade_tariffs`: tariffs, quotas, trade deficits, dumping, reciprocal trade, trade agreements.
- `manufacturing_reshoring`: domestic production, factories, jobs, reshoring, Buy American claims.
- `corporate_investment_deal`: company investments, acquisitions, joint ventures, announced projects.
- `diplomacy_negotiation`: meetings, summits, negotiations, state visits, personal diplomacy.
- `security_defense`: alliances, troops, burden sharing, missiles, nuclear weapons, sanctions.
- `campaign_domestic_politics`: electioneering, opponents, endorsements, domestic partisan framing.
- `regulation_environment`: emissions, permitting, environmental rules, industrial regulation.
- `market_price_supply`: prices, shortages, capacity, imports, demand, supply-chain conditions.
- `other`: relevant but not captured above; explain in `evidence_note`.

## Policy instruments

- `tariff`, `quota`, `antidumping_countervailing`, `sanction`
- `subsidy_tax_credit`, `procurement`, `investment_commitment`
- `trade_agreement`, `diplomatic_negotiation`, `military_posture`
- `regulatory_change`, `rhetorical_claim`, `none_or_unclear`

## Related industries

- `steel_primary`: mills, raw steel, flat/long products, stainless and specialty steels.
- `automotive`: vehicles and parts.
- `shipbuilding_maritime`: shipyards, commercial vessels, naval construction.
- `defense_aerospace`: weapons, aircraft, defense production.
- `semiconductors_electronics`: chips, fabs, electronics.
- `batteries_ev`: cells, battery materials, electric vehicles.
- `energy`: oil, gas, electricity, renewables, nuclear energy.
- `construction_infrastructure`: buildings, bridges, pipelines, public works.
- `mining_raw_materials`: iron ore, coal, scrap, critical minerals.
- `machinery_appliances`: industrial equipment and household appliances.
- `logistics_ports`: freight, ports, shipping services.
- `finance_investment`: financing, capital markets, sovereign or corporate investment.
- `agriculture_food`: agricultural trade or food products.
- `cross_industry_trade`: economy-wide trade claim with no defensible narrower industry.
- `none_or_unclear`: no industry connection supported by the post.

## Stance and tone

Choose one primary label and explain close calls:

- `supportive`, `critical`, `threatening_or_coercive`, `transactional`
- `celebratory`, `neutral_informational`, `campaigning`, `ambiguous`

Stance toward a country, company, and policy can differ. State the target in `evidence_note` when that distinction matters.
